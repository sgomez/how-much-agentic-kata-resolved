# Impostor

Impostor mode: who the impostor of each round is, the vote to find them, and its verdict.

## Language

**Impostor**: The role a player holds for one round of a game in impostor mode: the one player chosen at random for that round. What the impostor sees of the round is defined in the [Rounds glossary](../rounds/GLOSSARY.md). A role in a round, not in the whole game, and not a property of an estimate. _Avoid_: Spy, traitor, blind player

**Voting**: The moment after a round is revealed when players vote for who they think the impostor was. The host opens it; it ends when every player has voted or when the host closes it. _Avoid_: Poll, accusation, discussion

**Vote**: The player someone points at as the impostor. Every player votes, the impostor included; nobody votes for themselves, and a vote is final. _Avoid_: Accusation, suspicion, guess

**Verdict**: The outcome of a round's voting: caught or escaped. _Avoid_: Result, outcome, ruling

**Caught**: The verdict when the impostor alone has the most votes. _Avoid_: Found, unmasked, discovered

**Escaped**: The verdict when someone else has the most votes, when the most votes are tied, or when nobody votes. _Avoid_: Survived, not found, got away
