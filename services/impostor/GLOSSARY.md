# Impostor

Impostor mode: who the impostor of each round is, the vote to find them, and its verdict.

## Language

**Impostor**: The role a player holds for one round of a game in impostor mode: the one candidate chosen at random for that round, whether or not they were the impostor of an earlier round. What the impostor sees of the round is defined in the [Rounds glossary](../rounds/GLOSSARY.md). A role in a round, not in the whole game, and not a property of an estimate. _Avoid_: Spy, traitor, blind player

**Candidate**: A player who takes part in a round's voting: every player of the round when it was created, the host and the impostor included. Only candidates can be voted for, and every candidate votes but the impostor. _Avoid_: Participant, voter

**Voting**: The moment after a round is revealed, solution included, when candidates vote for who they think the impostor was. It is pending until the host opens it, which the host can only do once the round is revealed. It is over when every candidate but the impostor has voted or when the host closes it, and it never opens again. _Avoid_: Poll, accusation, discussion

**Vote**: The candidate someone points at as the impostor. Every candidate votes but the impostor, whose vote would always go to an innocent and help them escape; nobody votes for themselves, and a vote is final. _Avoid_: Accusation, suspicion, guess

**Verdict**: The outcome of a round's voting: caught or escaped. _Avoid_: Result, outcome, ruling

**Caught**: The verdict when the impostor alone has the most votes. _Avoid_: Found, unmasked, discovered

**Escaped**: The verdict when someone else has the most votes, when the most votes are tied, or when nobody votes. _Avoid_: Survived, not found, got away
