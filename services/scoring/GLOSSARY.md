# Scoring

The estimates players send and the points they earn: who came closest, who hit the solution, and how the scoreboard adds up over a game.

## Language

### Estimates

**Estimate**: The number a player sends for a round, inside the question's ruler range. A player can replace it until the deadline; the last one counts. A player who sends nothing has no estimate. _Avoid_: Answer, guess, bet

**Distance**: How far an estimate is from the solution, as an absolute difference. Going over and going under count the same. _Avoid_: Error, ratio, gap

### Points

**Closest**: The estimate, or the estimates tied at the same distance, nearest to the solution. Each scores 3 points; every other estimate scores none. The impostor's estimate competes like any other. _Avoid_: Winner, best, first place

**Exact hit**: An estimate equal to the solution, with no margin and no rounding. It scores 2 points on top of the 3 for the closest. _Avoid_: Bullseye, perfect, exact answer

**Scoreboard**: Every player of a game with the points earned so far, added up over its revealed rounds and the verdicts of its finished votings. Players without points appear with 0. _Avoid_: Ranking, leaderboard, standings, score
